The problem

Around half of technology transfers hit quality problems, and moving to an external site adds close to six months — because the thing that actually makes the process work was never in the package.

Sources. The ~50% figure was reported by Dr Uwe Hanenberg, Head of Product Implementation at Recipharm, from a CDMO Live 2025 roundtable, in PharmaSource, 22 May 2025 — a practitioner estimate from roundtable discussion, not a formal study. The lead-time figure is 5.8 months: “transfers to an external party take on average 5.8 months longer,” McKinsey & Company, 23 July 2020.

Comparability, pre-computed

Comparability under ICH Q5E is one of the largest time sinks in a transfer. Ideonix predicts which quality attributes are most likely to drift given this specific equipment and scale change, so the protocol is designed against the real risks instead of against everything.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does Ideonix do?

Ideonix turns a biomanufacturing process into a computable model — design space, equipment sensitivity and failure history — so the receiving site can interrogate it before the first GMP batch.

What is the Process Model?

The Process Model is a computable representation of a manufacturing process: unit operations and topology, critical process parameters at their real operating ranges rather than their filed ones, parameter interactions, scale-sensitive steps and observed failure modes. Every assertion is linked back to the batch, document or dataset it came from.

Where does Ideonix get its data?

From the record a site has already generated: development reports, batch records, deviation investigations, CAPAs, method transfer reports and historian traces. Every deviation is an experiment the process ran on itself at its own edges, and those investigations are the most information-dense and least-read documents in the building.

How does Ideonix predict where a transfer will break?

It maps the equipment delta between the sending and receiving sites — bioreactor geometry, mixing and oxygen transfer, hold capability, single-use versus stainless configuration — then models how critical parameters shift under those specific deltas and returns a ranked list of where the transfer will break, before the first engineering batch.

Does Ideonix replace our batch record or quality system?

No. Ideonix is decision support, not a GMP system of record. It does not release batches, replace the batch record or make quality decisions. Outputs are reviewed and approved through your existing quality system, exactly as an internal assessment would be.

How does Ideonix handle validation and regulatory requirements?

The platform is designed for GAMP 5 categorisation with 21 CFR Part 11 controls and a full audit trail. Where a Process Model informs a regulatory submission it does so as documented rationale supporting a human-authored argument, never as an authority.

How is our process data protected?

Single-tenant deployment where it is required, customer-managed encryption keys, role-based access scoped to the program, and a hard architectural line between your process data and the shared archetype library. There is no cross-customer model training without explicit contractual opt-in, and molecule identity is never shared.

Who is Ideonix for, and how does an engagement start?

The buyer is the Head of MSAT at clinical-stage biotech, large pharma tech ops and CDMOs. The entry engagement is a fixed-fee retrospective on a transfer you have already completed: give us a transfer that already failed, and we will produce the risk forecast we would have produced before it started so you can compare it to what actually happened.
